1. The service

Brainrot Maroc Video Creator (“the Tool”) is an automation workflow operated by the individual or business that installed it. It drafts one short-form vertical video concept per day, renders video clips with an AI video model, produces a voiceover script, caption and hashtags, and delivers the result to the connected TikTok account through TikTok’s official Content Posting API.
